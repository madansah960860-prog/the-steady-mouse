Contact The Steady Mouse

By the The Steady Mouse Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We are glad you want to get in touch, and we read every message ourselves. The best and only way to reach us is by email — there is no form to wrestle with and no account to create.

Email us

Write to notes@thesteadymouse.com. Tell us which guide you were reading and what you were trying to do, and we will point you to the clearest answer. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted a mistake on a page? Our editors welcome corrections at corrections@thesteadymouse.com.

What we can and cannot do

We are a small team writing free guides, so an email has limits — and some things we will never do.

  • We can explain a step more clearly, suggest the right guide, or fix an error on a page.
  • We cannot see or connect to your computer, and we would never ask to. We offer no repairs and no paid help.
  • We will never telephone you, never ask for a password, and never ask for payment. Anyone who does, claiming to be us, is an impostor.

Common questions

Is The Steady Mouse free?
Completely. Every guide is free, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments.
Can you fix my printer or computer remotely?
No. We publish reading material only and have no way to reach your equipment. For hands-on help, a trusted local shop or the maker's official website is the right place.
I got a call from “The Steady Mouse.” Was it you?
No — we never telephone anyone. That is a scam; please hang up and, if you wish, report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
Will you cover more topics?
Yes, over time. Tell us what trips you up and it may well become our next guide.

Safety reminder

No honest company phones out of the blue to ask for payment, passwords, or access to your computer. If a call or pop-up does, it is a scam — hang up or close it.

Report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
