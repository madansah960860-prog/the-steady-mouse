Privacy Policy

By the The Steady Mouse Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 6, 2026. The following privacy policy governs your use of The Steady Mouse.

Your privacy matters to us at The Steady Mouse, and the simplest way to protect it is to collect almost nothing — which is exactly what we do. This page explains, in plain language, what little we handle and the choices you have.

What we collect

The Steady Mouse does not require you to create an account, and it does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind. The only personal information we ever receive is the information you choose to send us — for example, the content of an email you write to notes@thesteadymouse.com.

When you email The Steady Mouse, we see your email address and whatever you include in your message. We read it only to answer you, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Preferences stored on your device

The Steady Mouse saves two small preferences in your browser's own local storage: the text size you pick with the "Text size" control, and whether you have dismissed the storage-notice bar. These stay on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

We do not sell or share your information

The Steady Mouse has never sold personal information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. There is no advertising network, no third-party pixel, and no social login anywhere on thesteadymouse.com.

Your privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. The Steady Mouse honors these rights for every reader, wherever you live:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected, used, or disclosed.
  • The right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — which does not apply here, because we do nothing of the kind.
  • The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • The right not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Because the only personal information we ever hold is an email you sent us, exercising most of these rights is as simple as writing to notes@thesteadymouse.com and asking us to delete that message.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The Steady Mouse is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, please write to notes@thesteadymouse.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how The Steady Mouse handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect. We encourage you to glance at this page now and then.

Contact us

Questions about privacy can go to notes@thesteadymouse.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

The Steady Mouse

The Steady Mouse is an independent educational publication for home computer users. We write calm, jargon-free help for everyday computer problems. We sell nothing and offer no services.

Advice is general and for learning only; steps vary by brand and version. The Steady Mouse is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company mentioned.
