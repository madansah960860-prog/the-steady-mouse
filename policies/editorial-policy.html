Editorial Policy

By the The Steady Mouse Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 6, 2026. This editorial policy covers everything published on the The Steady Mouse website.

This page explains how The Steady Mouse chooses topics, checks facts, and corrects mistakes. Our goal is simple: accurate, genuinely useful reading you can trust.

How we choose topics

At The Steady Mouse, we write about the everyday questions our readers actually ask. Topics come from reader emails, common search questions, and the gaps we notice in plain-language guidance.

Sources and accuracy

Where facts, figures, or procedures matter, The Steady Mouse relies on authoritative U.S. sources — government agencies, universities, and established nonprofits — and we link them so you can read the original. We state facts carefully and avoid claims we cannot support.

When sources disagree or a question is genuinely unsettled, we say so rather than pretend to a certainty we do not have. And when we simply cannot confirm a detail to our own satisfaction, we leave it out of The Steady Mouse entirely. We would always rather give you a little less information that we trust than more that we do not.

How we check facts

Before a guide appears on The Steady Mouse, someone other than its drafter reads it with a skeptical eye, checks any figures against the official source, and confirms that each step matches how things actually work. When a claim cannot be verified, we leave it out rather than guess. We would rather say less and be right than say more and be wrong.

Review dates

Each article on The Steady Mouse carries a "Last reviewed" date so you can see how current it is. We revisit guides periodically and update them when the facts change.

Corrections

We make mistakes like anyone, and we fix them openly. If you spot an error, write to corrections@thesteadymouse.com with the page and the problem, and we will review it and correct the page where needed.

Independence and no paid placements

The Steady Mouse accepts no payment to feature, recommend, or rank anything. There are no sponsored articles and no affiliate deals; our only aim is to be useful.

At The Steady Mouse, this independence is the quiet engine of everything else. Because no advertiser or sponsor can buy a place in our pages, we are free to leave something out when it is not useful, to recommend the free official option, and to tell you plainly when we are not the right source for a question. That freedom is worth more to us than any sponsorship.

AI assistance

Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing. A person is responsible for every page on The Steady Mouse.

Bylines

Articles are published under the byline "By the The Steady Mouse Editorial Team," which reflects how we work — collaboratively, with review before anything goes live.

Contact us

Editorial questions and corrections go to corrections@thesteadymouse.com.
